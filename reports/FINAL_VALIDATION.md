## What this validation does not establish

Finite arrays and exact replay establish computational consistency, not causal identification, monosemanticity, statistical power, robustness to random mapping seeds, or model-merging safety. There are only six independent wrapper clusters. Synthetic task shortcuts, low model competence, one random-map realization, pair-local multiplicity correction, and limited intervention/candidate coverage remain material. Supplementary natural-context and span-boundary measurements were verified for 5 models: seed11, seed29, qwen, smol, olmo. They remain descriptive diagnostics of frozen synthetic-task candidates on one author/book, not confirmatory cross-domain evidence.
